## 2. Summary

- The App does **not** require account creation or sign-in.  
- The App uses your chosen location source (device GPS or manually entered coordinates) to find nearby restaurants.  
- The App fetches place details (name, address, rating, photos, reviews) from **Google Places API** and may open links in **Google Maps**.  
- App settings and feedback preferences are stored **locally on your device** only.  
- The App shows ads through **Google AdMob** (banner and interstitial). Google may process data under its own policies.

## 3. Information the App processes

### 3.1 Data stored locally on your device

The App stores settings in app-local storage (for example, Godot `user://` data). This data is not sent to us by default:

- Search radius and location mode (use device location vs. manual coordinates)  
- Restaurant preference toggles and exclusion filters  
- Excluded restaurant names you add  
- Place "thumbs up/down" weighting preferences used to influence future picks  

Uninstalling the App typically removes this local data (subject to your device backup/restore behavior).

### 3.2 Location data

If you enable device location, the App requests location permission and reads your current coordinates to perform nearby search.  

If you disable device location, you may manually enter coordinates.  

Location values are used to retrieve results and compute approximate distance in the UI.

### 3.3 Network requests and third-party services

When you use the App, it can send requests to third-party services:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Google Places API** | Fetch nearby restaurants and place metadata (name, address, rating, user rating count, reviews, photos, map URI). |
| **Google Maps / maps URLs** | Open navigation/directions when you tap "Take me there". |
| **Google-hosted image endpoints** | Load place photos for result cards. |

These services receive standard technical request data (such as IP address, device/network metadata, and request timing) according to their own policies.

### 3.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses **Google AdMob** to show ads, including banner and interstitial ads.

Google may process identifiers (including advertising ID), device/app information, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, and protect ad services, and to personalize ads where allowed.

Please review:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)  

Depending on your region and device, you may be able to adjust ad personalization in your Google account and device Ads settings.
